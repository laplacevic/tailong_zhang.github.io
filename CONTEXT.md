# CONTEXT — tailongzhang.github.io

Glossary of terms for Tailong Zhang's personal academic website.

## Terms

### Academic calling card
The site's purpose. A static, public-facing profile that lets people who
encountered Tailong (at a conference, via a paper, via search) learn who he
is and how to reach him. Explicitly NOT a blog, not an interactive platform,
and has no server-side features. "Social networking" in the original brief
resolves to this meaning: the site carries links to external networks
(ORCID, GitHub, Bluesky) rather than hosting any networking features itself.

### Editorial style
The visual direction. Light background, typography-driven layout in the
Swiss/editorial tradition: large serif display headings, restrained sans-serif
body text, strict grid, generous whitespace. Exoplanet imagery is limited to a
single decorative motif — a transmission-spectrum curve — rather than starfield
or planet illustrations.

### Restrained motion
The agreed animation budget. Entrance reveals (staggered text rise, spectrum
curve draw-in), scroll-triggered section fades, and designed hover states.
No scroll-driven storytelling, no continuous ambient animation. All motion
respects the visitor's reduced-motion preference.

### Research description
The public phrasing of Tailong's research: transmission-spectroscopy retrieval
of exoplanet atmospheres in the JWST/Ariel era, with interests in minor
molecules (SO2, NH3, H2S, HCN) and atmospheric chemistry as context for planet
formation. Deliberately avoids framing any work as inversion from spectra to
formation history or interior structure (supervisor constraint).

### Talks/News
The single timeline section listing talks given and notable events. Stands in
for a Publications section until there are publications worth listing; an
empty Publications page is considered worse than none.
