# PRODUCT.md — tailongzhang.github.io

## Product Purpose

Personal academic website for Tailong Zhang, PhD student in exoplanet
atmospheres at King's College London (supervisor: Giovanna Tinetti).
An academic calling card: a static, single-page profile that lets people
who encountered him (conference, paper, search) learn who he is, what he
works on, and how to reach him. It links out to external networks (ORCID,
GitHub, email); it hosts no interactive features itself.

## Register

brand

Design IS the product here: the site is a portfolio/identity surface,
not a tool.

## Users

- Researchers and faculty who met Tailong at a talk or conference and
  want to place him: institution, supervisor, research direction.
- Potential collaborators checking credibility before replying to an email.
- Reviewers, summer-school organizers, fellowship committees doing a
  quick background look.

They arrive on a laptop in a bright office or conference hall, spend
under two minutes, and leave with: name, face, one-line research
identity, and a contact route.

## Tone

Quietly confident, precise, academic without stuffiness. The voice of a
careful scientist, not a startup founder. Every sentence earns its place.
Restraint signals seriousness; one well-executed flourish (the spectrum
motif) signals craft.

## Anti-references

- Hugo Blox / al-folio / Jekyll academic templates: the thousand
  identical academic sites with sidebar photo + publication list.
- Dark "space site" aesthetic: starfields, planet renders, neon-on-black.
  Exoplanet imagery is allowed only as annotated scientific figures in
  the editorial language (Figs. 1-3: spectrum, formation-and-migration,
  transit); never illustration or photography.
- SaaS landing-page tropes: gradient blobs, hero metrics, card grids.

## Strategic principles

- Looks intentional at zero content volume: the site must feel complete
  with one talk and no papers.
- Maintained by a non-frontend-dev (Python researcher): plain content
  files, no dependency churn, builds for years.
- Motion is restrained: entrance reveals, scroll fades, designed hover
  states; nothing continuous. (Owner chose to drop reduced-motion
  gating, 2026-07.)
- Static output, GitHub Pages, fast on conference wifi.
